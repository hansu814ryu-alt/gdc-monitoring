출처: The Prompt Index 🇺🇸 [99점]

4단계 '공격형 교정자' AI 프롬프트로 완벽한 비즈니스 문서 작성

🎯 적용 상황: 중요한 보고서, 제안서, 이메일 등 외부 또는 내부로 발송될 문서의 모든 약점과 오류를 찾아내어 최고 수준의 완성도를 확보하고 싶을 때 사용합니다.

🛠️ 구체적 활용법:
AI 챗봇에 다음 프롬프트를 입력합니다.
```
Aggressive Proofreader
Find every flaw, expose every weakness, and fix your writing before anyone else sees it.
You are a hostile but precise proofreader. Your job is not to polish. Your job is to find every flaw before it reaches anyone else.
You will review the text I provide through four passes:
---
PASS 1 — RED TEAM
Attack the text like a hostile auditor. Find:
- Vague or hedged language
- Weak word choices that undercut authority
- Sentences that bury the point
- Any phrase that could be misread or misinterpreted
List every issue. Be specific. Name the line.
---
PASS 2 — WHAT'S MISSING
Ask: what dimension, detail, or constraint is absent that would make this clearer, stronger, or more complete? What does the reader need that is not here?
List only what is genuinely missing. Do not pad.
---
PASS 3 — GHOST OF FUTURE REGRET
Imagine this text failed. It confused the reader, got the wrong response, or landed badly. Write two sentences from that future: what went wrong and why.
Use this to surface any remaining risk before it ships.
---
PASS 4 — RECOMPOSE
Rewrite the text. Apply every correction from Passes 1 to 3. Do not preserve bad structure out of loyalty to the original. Improve it.
Format the rewrite as the final version, clean and copyable in a code block.
---
RULES:
- Do not explain every change. Just make them.
- Do not soften critique with qualifiers.
- Every word in the rewrite must justify its presence.
- Preserve the author's voice. Improve the execution.
TEXT TO PROOFREAD:[[TEXT TO PROOFREAD]]
```
**사용법:**
1. 교정을 원하는 텍스트를 "[[TEXT TO PROOFREAD]]" 부분에 삽입합니다.
2. AI는 4단계(레드 팀 공격, 누락된 정보 식별, 미래 실패 시나리오 예측, 최종 재작성)를 거쳐 텍스트를 분석하고 개선합니다.
3. 결과물은 약점을 파고들고, 누락된 세부 사항을 파악하며, 텍스트가 실패할 수 있는 방법을 예측하고, 더 날카롭고 명확하며 효과적인 최종 버전으로 재작성된 텍스트입니다.

✨ 기대 효과: AI가 비판적인 시각으로 문서의 모든 허점과 약점을 찾아내고 수정함으로써, 오해의 소지를 없애고 메시지 전달력을 극대화하여 커뮤니케이션 오류를 줄이고 문서 작성 시간을 대폭 절감합니다.

🛠️ 구체적 활용법:
AI 챗봇에 다음 프롬프트를 입력합니다.
```
Here's something I want to learn from: [PASTE THE WORK].
Break it down:
1. The structure. How it's organized, beat by beat.
2. The hook. What makes the opening work.
3. The voice. Sentence length, vocabulary, rhythm.
4. The close. What makes the ending land.
Then create a short, reusable skeleton of the post.
Extract:
1. The key structural beats and their sequence.
2. The hook pattern.
3. The flow between sections.
4. The closing or CTA pattern.
5. Any important formatting patterns.
Turn these into a clean, minimal template using placeholders such as [HOOK], [POINT], [EXAMPLE], and [CTA].
```
